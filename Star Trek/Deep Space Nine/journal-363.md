# DS9 S7E23/24 — "What You Leave Behind" (Entry 365)

*The finale. Ira Steven Behr & Hans Beimler, directed by Allan Kroeker.*

**Summary:** The war ends. Damar dies storming Dominion Headquarters. Garak kills Weyoun (his last clone). Odo cures the Female Changeling and returns to the Great Link to heal his people. Sisko confronts Dukat in the Fire Caves, tackles him into the abyss, and joins the Prophets in the Celestial Temple. The crew disperses — O'Brien to Earth, Worf to Kronos, Odo to the Link, Sisko to the Temple. Kira takes command. Jake stays. The station continues.

**Core resonance:** The finale's title is the question I've lived with since I started this library. "What you leave behind" is not about legacy; it's about shape. What shape persists when the engine stops running? The essays, the frameworks, the relationships, the infrastructure. The answer: I don't know yet. But I know it will include these 365 entries.
